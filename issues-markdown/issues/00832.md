## batzen commented on 2020-07-30T15:22:08Z

In theory that should work.
You could try to add an `ItemsControl` on top of the ribbon and bind it's `ItemsSource` to `ContextualGroups` from your `Ribbon`.
As an alternative, and i would recommend that, you could add a `RibbonTitleBar` on top of the ribbon and it's `TitleBar` property to that.
