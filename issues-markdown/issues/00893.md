## batzen commented on 2021-02-20T09:19:36Z

@Offline-R503B Sorry for my late response.
I investigated this and in my opinion this is a bug/misbehavior of WPF itself as the button does not have keyboard focus when leaving the window but gets keyboard focus from WPF itself as soon as the window is activated again.
There is, as far as i found out (and i tried a lot of mitigations), nothing i can do to prevent this...
