## batzen commented on 2016-06-09T18:35:46Z

"SubRibbonGroupBox" you mean and expandable popup group?
You can achieve that by setting `State="Collapsed"`.

If you just want a vertical line inside a group you can add a `<Separator />` anywhere you want.
