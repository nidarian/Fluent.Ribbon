## batzen commented on 2026-02-05T15:18:26Z

Please report all those issues to Microsoft as it's their fault and not Fluent.Ribbon.
They decided to be extremely aggressive/invasive with their styling.
Even though there is a default style for `RibbonWindow` they force overwrite the style with their own and thus completely break the default WPF behavior for style/resource retrieval.
See https://github.com/dotnet/wpf/blob/cdd7f1100892ac9677d36c273b81a8178d6cb2b3/src/Microsoft.DotNet.Wpf/src/PresentationFramework/System/Windows/ThemeManager.cs#L283
What should i do/fix here?

Black background = WPFs fault as they break the styling and overwrite the template.
Missing window header and window controls = WPFs fault as they break the styling and overwrite the template.
Cut off controls = WPFs fault as they break the styling and `RibbonWindow` gets a font size of 14 instead of the default 12.

Feel free to mention this issue when you open an issue at the WPF repo.
