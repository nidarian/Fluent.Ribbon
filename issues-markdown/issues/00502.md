How to set <Fluent:RibbonGroupBox> items alignment?
I wanna set TextBox in the center of GroupBox:
` <Fluent:RibbonGroupBox  Header="Filter" >
                    <Fluent:TextBox  Header="Name"
                                        Width="150"
                                        HorizontalAlignment="Center"
                                        VerticalAlignment="Center"
                                        />
                </Fluent:RibbonGroupBox>`
But it doesn't work. How item's alignment can be changed?

## batzen commented on 2018-01-17T18:42:39Z

The items panel for RibbonGroupBox is a WrapPanel so your best bet would be to use a Grid around your Fluent:TextBox and adjust layout settings on that one and on the textbox.

Using layout settings on the textbox is problematic because ribbon controls, like Fluent:TextBox, are subject to custom layout schemes because they get resized etc. and that's one thing you will loose by wrapping it in a grid. They TextBox will no longer be resized to Large, Middle or Small.

Does that answer your question?
