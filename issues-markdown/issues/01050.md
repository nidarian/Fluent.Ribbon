## batzen commented on 2022-08-25T16:54:10Z

You have to include the XAML resource from Fluent.Ribbon somewhere.
The best place, most of the time, is your `App.xaml`.
Just add:
```
<Application.Resources>
    <ResourceDictionary>
        <ResourceDictionary.MergedDictionaries>
            <ResourceDictionary Source="pack://application:,,,/Fluent;component/Themes/Generic.xaml" />
        </ResourceDictionary.MergedDictionaries>
    </ResourceDictionary>
</Application.Resources>
```
