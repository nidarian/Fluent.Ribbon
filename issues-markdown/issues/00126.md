## batzen commented on 2015-06-15T16:22:07Z

The property you are looking for is not called IsCollapsed but IsMinimized instead.
To disable minimizing you would have to search for "PART_MinimizeButton" in the RibbonTabControl. There is currently no easier way to disable minimizing the ribbon.
