## batzen commented on 2017-08-31T16:44:16Z

`IsButtonEnabled="False"` disables the button part.
`IsEnabled="False"` disables the whole `SplitButton`.

But you are right, if the command can't be executed the button is still enabled.
Will have a look at that.
