I have a gallery with ItemsSource bound on some collection property. When I replace the collection (not adding or removing items but completely change the collection reference) a new item in the collection is not displayed.

The original collection has 2 items. When I show the gallery I see the two items. When I change the collection for another one that has 3 items, the third item is not visible. The binding works (I see the notification and the get on the property happen). Also when I look into the visual tree, the gallery now has 3 GalleryItem. However the gallery does not resize to accomodate the new item.

What am I missing here?
