## batzen commented on 2018-08-29T15:04:29Z

Theming got a lot better in version 7.0 these things.
It's currently available as a preview version on nuget.

To change the selected foreground when your item is not part of a contextual group you can overwrite the resource `Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.RibbonTabItem.Selected.Foreground`.
For mouse over foreground you can overwrite `Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.RibbonTabItem.MouseOver.Foreground`.

When your tab is inside a contextual group you can use the properties `TabItemSelectedMouseOverForeground`, `TabItemMouseOverForeground` and `TabItemSelectedForeground` on the group.
