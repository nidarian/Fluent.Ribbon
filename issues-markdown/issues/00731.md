## batzen commented on 2019-07-12T15:23:40Z

It creates a new `BackstageTabControl` for you because you told it to do so by adding a `BackstageTabControl` to the `StartScreen`.
Just move ` ItemsPanelMinWidth="200" ItemsPanelBackground="Orange"` to `StartScreenTabControl` and remove the `BackstageTabControl`.

---

## batzen commented on 2019-07-12T15:53:33Z

And you can also use `LeftContentMargin` on the `StartScreenTabControl` to remove the margin.
