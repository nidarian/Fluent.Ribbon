## batzen commented on 2019-07-11T16:27:14Z

I think that's the regular behavior for regular tooltips.
Once you move the mouse over a control that has a tooltip wpf starts a timer (which is controlled by `ToolTipService.InitialShowDelay`).
Once that timer fires the tooltip is opened at some point around the position the mouse had at some point in time during that delay.
