Steps to reproduce:

1. Open RibbonWindow with fluent ribbon control.
2. Double click on window title bar to maximize it (works ok)
3. Double click on window title bar to restore it (also works ok)
4. Double click again on restored window title bar. It should maximize as before, but this time maximizing it breaks the title bar (you can see my desktop background where upper part of title bar should be)

![obraz](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/10825459/43075499-603c6554-8e81-11e8-9765-6b5e8ed96b32.png)

Moving the window anywhere between steps 3 and 4 fixes the issue for the next double click.

### Environment

- Fluent.Ribbon __v6.1.0__
- Windows 10
- .NET Framework 4.6
