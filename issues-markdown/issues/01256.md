## batzen commented on 2025-12-19T19:38:13Z

You have to change the value of `Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.GroupSeparator.Background`.
You can find a list of all resources at https://github.com/fluentribbon/Fluent.Ribbon/blob/develop/ReferenceData/vNextResourceKeys.txt
For a colorized sample have a look at https://github.com/fluentribbon/Fluent.Ribbon/blob/develop/Fluent.Ribbon.Showcase/RibbonWindowColorized.xaml
