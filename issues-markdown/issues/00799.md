## kalatchev commented on 2020-05-19T20:54:45Z

Thanks, @batzen.  

Setting 
```XAML
<Fluent:RibbonWindow x:Class="MainWindow"
   ...
   GlowBrush="{x:Null}">
</Fluent:RibbonWindow>
```
solves the problem! I can't say why, but obviously ```GlowBrush``` somehow causes mentioned slowness.

Thanks again for the tip!

## batzen commented on 2020-05-20T21:35:22Z

> Just a detail - I think I have some problems with a driver of my video card. It may be the reason that in my case slowness is much worst that in ussual case.

That really seems to be the case here.
As you said setting `GlowBrush` to `null` solved your issue.
The main regular performance bottleneck caused by the glow windows is setting/updating their window positions. In the version of ControlzEx you are using the windows positions were still updated because we forgot to if case those.
This means your issue is caused by the rendering of the half transparent glows around the window which happens through the GPU.
