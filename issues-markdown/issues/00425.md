## batzen commented on 2017-04-14T15:59:12Z

Because you are trying exactly that, setting the content twice.
Your first content is `<Fluent:Ribbon>` and the second is `<Grid>` you have to do something like:
```
<Grid>
    <Grid.RowDefinitions>
        <RowDefinition Height="Auto" />
        <RowDefinition Height="*" />
    </Grid.RowDefinitions>
    <Fluent:Ribbon Grid.Row="0">
        ....
    </Fluent:Ribbon>
    <Grid Grid.Row="1">
       <TextBlock>My first window containing a Ribbon and something else.</TextBlock>
    </Grid>
</Grid>
```
