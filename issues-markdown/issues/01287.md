## batzen commented on 2026-10-21T18:43:39Z

That's the default design, as the content should steer the width and not the header.

If you want something different you'll have to either overwrite the HeaderTemplateSelector of your GroupBox(es) or overwrite the DataTemplates with the key Fluent.Ribbon.DataTemplates.RibbonGroupBox.OneLineHeader.

---

## batzen commented on 2026-10-21T19:08:44Z

And, depending on your needs, you would have to lift the MaxWidth restriction of the template part PART_DownGrid.
Keep in mind that changing the template and lifting the width restriction will cause the GroupBox to change state or shrink items way earlier than required by it's hosted controls.
