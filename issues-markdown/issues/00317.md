My class library is called LRIL.Images


---

## batzen commented on 2016-05-11T12:27:31Z

`LargeIcon="pack://application:,,,/LRIL.Images;component/Images/Exit.png"`

With "Images/Exit.png" being replaced by your resource-path should work perfectly.
