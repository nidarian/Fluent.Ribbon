## batzen commented on 2016-11-05T13:46:14Z

There is currently no blue theme for office 2013.

My plan was to introduce new color schemes in a version after 5.0, but i got no schedule for the color schemes.

You could also try to create your own color scheme.
Basically you would only need to overwrite the colors which are present in `Colors.xaml`.
Some colors currently can't be overwritten because they are hard coded in some styles/templates.
I will improve that situation over time, but as i am fine with the current color scheme it's not on top of my todo list.
