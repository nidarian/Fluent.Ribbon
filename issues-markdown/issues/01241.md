## batzen commented on 2025-03-29T04:56:08Z

Your Winforms parts seems to have issues when Mica is used as a backdrop.
You can prevent the issue by setting `controlzex:WindowBackdropManager.BackdropType="None"` on your WPF window.
The issue gets more visible when you use `controlzex:WindowBackdropManager.BackdropType="Auto"`.
