## znakeeye commented on 2018-11-29T14:46:06Z

I analyzed this a bit further. Something changed between `v3.6` and `v7`.

**v3.6** has a `Grid` containing both `Part_TitleBar`, `PART_ContentPresenter` and `PART_WindowCommands` - all placed at `Grid.Row="0"`. Thus, the content will indeed place itself at the top.

**v7** has a `DockPanel` for the caption bar and below it there is this `PART_ContentPresenter`. Thus, the content will be placed below the caption bar.

**Is this change by design?**

---

## batzen commented on 2018-11-29T15:14:01Z

This was changed in version 5.0.0 with https://github.com/fluentribbon/Fluent.Ribbon/issues/349 to allow usage of `Ribbon` outside of a `RibbonWindow`.
