## batzen commented on 2018-08-18T17:47:03Z

That's because the ribbon itself is the lowest layer and there are quite a few layers above the ribbon.
Have a look at https://github.com/fluentribbon/Fluent.Ribbon/blob/develop/Fluent.Ribbon.Showcase/RibbonWindowColorized.xaml to get a feeling for which colors you can/should override to get your desired effect.
Feel free to ask any questions along your way.
