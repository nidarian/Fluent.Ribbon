## batzen commented on 2021-01-23T14:47:50Z

@Offline-R503B Just had a look at this and finally remembered why this happens.
The Backstage has a margin for the content part so that the titlebar is still visible when it's open.
You can change the margin by changing `SelectedContentMargin` on the `BackstageTabControl`.
Setting it to 0 should solve your issue, but be aware that this causes the titlebar to be completely covered when the backstage is open.
