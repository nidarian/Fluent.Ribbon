## batzen commented on 2018-04-19T11:36:13Z

A repro is, most of the times, better than a screenshot because i can see what you tried to do.

But you are right the groups are tied to `KeyboardNavigation.GetVisualRoot`. As i am just inheriting from the regular wpf `RadioButton` there is not much i can do to change that.

You will have to use code behind to be able to achieve what you want. You could, for example, don't add your buttons to a group (not assigning `GroupName` at all) and then handling the mutual exclusive selection in code behind.
