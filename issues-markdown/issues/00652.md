## batzen commented on 2018-10-07T18:17:40Z

I can't tell you the exact reason, but it might be caused by your custom window.
In general i made very negative experiences with including resources that should be global at the window level.
This would/should also remove the need to forward the window resources to your FileManagerControl.
Moving 
```
<ResourceDictionary Source="pack://application:,,,/RibbonFileManager;component/LightPlexBlue.xaml"/>
```
from the window to app.xaml fixes your issue.

By the way you should add 
```
Directory.CreateDirectory(Path.GetDirectoryName(_favoritesPath));
```
at line 35 of your Manager class, otherwise the app crashes on startup. ;-)
