## batzen commented on 2015-03-31T19:50:37Z

Currently, and that's one of the problems with that solution, you have to place it in your App.xaml or in one of your own ResourceDictionary (which has to be loaded after the Fluent.Ribbon ResourceDictionary).

This means that you can only set one Color for your whole application and not per Window.

Example for App.xaml (just modified the one from our showcase application):

```
<Application xmlns="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml/presentation"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:Fluent="urn:fluent-ribbon"
             x:Class="FluentTest.App"
             StartupUri="TestWindow.xaml">
    <Application.Resources>
        <ResourceDictionary>
            <ResourceDictionary.MergedDictionaries>
                <!--<ResourceDictionary Source="pack://application:,,,/Fluent;Component/Themes/Generic.xaml" />-->
                <ResourceDictionary Source="pack://application:,,,/Fluent;Component/Themes/Office2013/Generic.xaml" />
                <!--<ResourceDictionary Source="pack://application:,,,/Fluent;Component/Themes/Windows8/Generic.xaml" />-->
            </ResourceDictionary.MergedDictionaries>

            <Color x:Key="{x:Static Fluent:MetroColors.ThemeColorKey}">Red</Color>
        </ResourceDictionary>
    </Application.Resources>
</Application>
```
