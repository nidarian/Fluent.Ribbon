## batzen commented on 2020-04-11T19:58:31Z

In order to use that shortcut syntax for images/icons in Fluent.Ribbon you have to either copy your images to a folder in your output path or embed them as resources.
Otherwise there is no way to resolve those images/icons.

## batzen commented on 2020-04-12T06:47:03Z

You could try adding the following to your project file:
```
<ItemGroup>
    <Resource Include="images/*.png" />
</ItemGroup>
```
That should do the trick to get your images embedded as resources.
