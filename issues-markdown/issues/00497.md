## batzen commented on 2018-01-15T10:13:12Z

You mean on the left side of the window? That's currently only possible by adding items to the `QuickAccessToolBar` itself.
However you can add items to the right side by either adding items to `Ribbon.ToolBarItems` (that would place the items to right of the minimize ribbon button) or by adding items to `RibbonWindow.WindowCommands` (that would place the items to the left of the minimize etc. buttons).
