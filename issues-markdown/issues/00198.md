## chdft commented on 2015-11-14T13:12:36Z

Just to make sure that I get that right: you want to add a button to your ribbon which disables the ribbon when it is clicked and keeps it disabled until you re-enable it programmatically.
If that is the case, you can use a `Fluent:ToggleButton` and its `IsChecked` property. That results in something like that:

```
    <Fluent:RibbonWindow.Resources>
        <Style x:Key="rbnDisabled"
               TargetType="{x:Type Fluent:Ribbon}">
            <Setter Property="IsEnabled"
                    Value="True" />
            <Style.Triggers>
                <DataTrigger Binding="{Binding ElementName=g3b2, Path=IsChecked}"
                             Value="True">
                    <Setter Property="IsEnabled"
                            Value="False"></Setter>
                </DataTrigger>
            </Style.Triggers>
        </Style>
    </Fluent:RibbonWindow.Resources>
    <Grid>
        <Fluent:Ribbon Style="{StaticResource rbnDisabled}">
            <!--Tabs-->
            <Fluent:RibbonTabItem Header="Tab">
                <Fluent:RibbonGroupBox Header="Group">
                    <Fluent:ToggleButton Name="g3b2"
                                   IsChecked="False"
                                   Header="Green" />
                </Fluent:RibbonGroupBox>
            </Fluent:RibbonTabItem>
        </Fluent:Ribbon>
    </Grid>
```

Please note, that this will disable the ribbon and all controls within the ribbon. The `Fluent:ToggleButton` will be disabled (and therefore not clickable anymore) as well as a result, once it is clicked.
