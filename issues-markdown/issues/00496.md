## batzen commented on 2018-01-09T15:28:39Z

You can change the background of `StatusBar` by either setting it's `Background` property or by overwriting the resource `Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.AccentBaseColorBrush`. Overwriting `Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.AccentBaseColorBrush` however will cause that color/brush to be overwritten everywhere.

You can change the background of the titlebar by setting `TitleBackground` on `RibbonWindow`.
