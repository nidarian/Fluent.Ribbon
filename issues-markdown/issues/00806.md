## batzen commented on 2020-05-21T12:23:14Z

You can add any item you want to `RibbonWindow.WindowCommands`.
Items added there appear on the left side of the window buttons.
Or you can add any item to `Ribbon.ToolBarItems`.
Items added there appear on the right side of the ribbons minimize button.
