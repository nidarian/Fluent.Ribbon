# Issue #564: Gallery overflow panel (with menu items) doesn't close when clicking once in the application

- **State:** closed
- **Author:** PieterThielens
- **Created:** 2018-04-13T11:12:35Z
- **Closed:** 2018-04-15T18:47:35Z
- **Labels:** Bug :lady_beetle:

**Two mouse clicks in the application are needed to close the overflow panel of the gallery (with menu items)**

This reproducible in the ribbon showcase Galleries tab, open the overflow panel of the first gallery (the one that has two menu items).
When the panel is open move with the mouse over the menu items (so that they received focus), keep moving down and click in the application under the pane. 
The panel does not close, click again and it will close.
