## abdelkarim commented on 2015-04-03T21:00:41Z

Hi @batzen 

First, don't try to fix the above issue, because I have prepared another Pull Request that will take care of it.

### Motivation

During my work on this suggestion Pull Request, I didn't understand the reason for the following:
![image](https://cloud.githubusercontent.com/assets/1153480/6987188/8e5a7d56-da3c-11e4-98b9-3d21243f8294.png)

I tried to optimize it directly by taking rid off all of this and just using a `ContentPresenter` with `HorizontalAlignment="Center"`, to my surprise what I did broke the end result.

What I understood is that everything as it is in the picture is needed to correctly position the header. I believe it is wrong for two things:
- This is not how items are centered in WPF, `HorizontalAlignment="Center"` should do the job.
- Users might get confused when they try to customize the look. I was.

## Solution

The solution I will discuss is up and running just fine, I will push it in a Pull Request right after this #89 is merged.

![image](https://cloud.githubusercontent.com/assets/1153480/6988631/3aea0538-da4b-11e4-8542-8bcd5364a5e2.png)

I took rid off the `RibbonTabItem.MeasureOverride` and the **PART_ContentContainer** dependency, they're no longer needed. The idea is to manipulate the `Padding` of the `RibbonTabItem` and the available space.
I mainly refactored the `RibbonTabsContainer` to:
1. Reduce the Padding value during re-size, we start with the value 17 on both the left and right, after this step we end at 5. 12 is for the default indent.
2. After that we start reducing the width of the control, by width I mean the available space reserved for the control.

For contextual tabs, we have three steps in some cases, because some tabs might be extended to accommodate the length of their enclosing Contextual Group, we reduce it during the second step above.

After you take a look at the final implementation, you will notice that what we have now is similar to what was there.

I talked briefly about my solution, it is already up and fully implemented in the [TabsContainerRewrite](https://github.com/abdelkarim/Fluent.Ribbon/tree/TabsContainerRewrite) branch. It is based on the changes in #89 .

Please take a look and give me some feedback before I push my changes in a Pull Request.
