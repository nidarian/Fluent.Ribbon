Hi!

I've added ribbon galleries to groups, using Fluent 8.0.3. The resizing of the galleries works fine. However, I the RibbonGroup does not collapse to a dropdown button:

![Gall](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/9028723/106322161-b1495180-6275-11eb-9e0c-9e09166f5a91.gif)

My assumption is that there is something wrong with the ReduceOrder I defined in XAML, but I cannot get it to behave correctly:

```xml
<fluent:RibbonTabItem Header="Tab1" ReduceOrder="FirstGalleryGroup,FirstGalleryGroup,FirstGalleryGroup,(SecondGalleryGroup),(SecondGalleryGroup),(SecondGalleryGroup),(ThirdGalleryGroup),(ThirdGalleryGroup),(ThirdGalleryGroup)">
  <fluent:RibbonGroupBox Name="FirstGalleryGroup" Header="Group1" ItemsSource="{Binding Group1Items}" />
  <fluent:RibbonGroupBox Name="SecondGalleryGroup" Header="Group2" ItemsSource="{Binding Group2Items}" />
  <fluent:RibbonGroupBox Name="ThirdGalleryGroup" Header="Group3" ItemsSource="{Binding Group3Items}" />
</fluent:RibbonTabItem>
```

Here's my small example project:

[FluentTest.zip](https://github.com/fluentribbon/Fluent.Ribbon/files/5896104/FluentTest.zip)

Can you give me a hint what's wrong?
