Hi!

I've updated to v11.0.2. 
I now see that in the backstage area there is no highlighting of the tab item background and also not for the selected item anymore.
The only highlighting that happens is with the thick border that appears on the left side of the tab.
Is this done intentionally?
I saw that there are still brushes defined in the different themes for mouse hover and selection background, but they do not seem to have an effect.

I know that this pretty much matches with the office style, but unfortunately it does not comply with our companies style guide.

## batzen commented on 2026-02-09T17:47:13Z

Hi.

It's intentional as I try to stay close to the current Office versions.
The new design was discussed in https://github.com/fluentribbon/Fluent.Ribbon/issues/957 since 2021.

All defined brushes should be used. Which ones aren't used? Please keep in mind that some, like `Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.RibbonTabItem.MouseOver.Background`, default to `Transparent` now.

> but unfortunately it does not comply with our companies style guide.

Which parts don't comply and what is blocking you from making the proper style/color adjustments to comply with your companies style guide?

I tried to overwrite the brush resources like this:
```
<!--  BackstageTabControl  -->
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.BackstageTabControl.Background" options:Freeze="True" Color="LightGray" />
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.BackstageTabControl.Button.MouseOver.Background" options:Freeze="True" Color="Red" /><!-- Unknown -->
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.BackstageTabControl.ItemsPanelBackground" options:Freeze="True" Color="Gray" />

<!--  BackstageTabItem  -->
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.BackstageTabItem.Header.Foreground" options:Freeze="True" Color="Black" />
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.BackstageTabItem.MouseOver.Background" options:Freeze="True" Color="Yellow" /><!-- Only affects background of back button -->
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.BackstageTabItem.Selected.Background" options:Freeze="True" Color="Cyan" /><!-- Vertical bar -->
```

I think the resource "Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.BackstageTabControl.Button.MouseOver.Background" is not used at all. 
I also changed "Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.BackstageTabItem.MouseOver.Background", but this only has an effect on the background of the back button.
I found out that the "Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.BackstageTabItem.Selected.Background" affects the brush of the vertical bar in front of the selected tab. I found it just a bit strange that this is named "...Selected.**Background**"

In order to completely fullfil my company's regulations, I would have to make the background of the tab header lighter on mouse hover and darker when it is selected. This clearly differs from the current MS Office behavior

## batzen commented on 2026-02-15T05:47:40Z

`Fluent.Ribbon.Brushes.BackstageTabControl.Button.MouseOver.Background` isn't used. I forgot to delete it.

To get exactly what you want you'll have to clone and modify `Fluent.Ribbon.Templates.BackstageTabItem`, which is about 100 lines plus the style, if you want to define colors/brushes there.
Would that be ok for you, or do you absolutely need Fluent.Ribbon to support this out of the box?
