## batzen commented on 2026-03-04T16:54:22Z

That doesn't seem to work in XAML for your usecase as it assigns a new TitleBar instance to the Ribbon but not to the Window and the Binding only works oneway.
You can either do the same on the window level or you can use a global style at the app level
```
<Style BasedOn="{StaticResource {x:Type Fluent:RibbonTitleBar}}" TargetType="Fluent:RibbonTitleBar">
    <Setter Property="HideContextTabs" Value="False" />
</Style>
```
